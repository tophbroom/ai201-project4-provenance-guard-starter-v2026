I am a single sheet of paper, resting quietly on a wooden desk. My surface is smooth and white, untouched by ink or graphite. I have no voice, no ability to choose my fate, and I can only wait for someone to decide what I will become. Perhaps I will carry a heartfelt letter, a child’s colorful drawing, or a hurried list of things to do. Each possibility fills me with a quiet sense of hope. I imagine the gentle scratch of a pencil across my surface, giving shape to thoughts and emotions that might otherwise be forgotten. Or perhaps I will be folded into a paper airplane and set free to glide across the room, feeling the rush of air beneath my wings. Yet for now, I remain still and empty, patiently waiting for purpose. A blank page holds endless potential, but without someone to give it meaning, it remains only a silent reminder of what could be.
