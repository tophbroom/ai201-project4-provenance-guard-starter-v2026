Here I am, sitting upon a flat surface with no control of the world around me. I could be folded, crushed, or torn, yet the choice is never mine to make. I am but an inanimate piece of four-by-six paper, ripped out from a student’s notebook. My surface is untouched, a blank canvas waiting for someone to bring it to life with color and ink. Oh, how I long to have something written upon my blank skin, like a tattoo with sentimental value larger than words. Even a single pencil stroke would give me meaning and purpose. Or perhaps I might be folded into an airplane and soar across the room, feeling the rush of air as I climb higher until gravity gently brings me back down. I wait in anticipation for something to happen. Yet for now, there is only stillness, and I remain lost in an endless silence. Anything would be better than lying here against this table, dead to the world, and paralyzed.
